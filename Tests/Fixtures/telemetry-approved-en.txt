Prompt, single player and Steam:

 Share combat data to help balance the game?

 If you say yes, this copy of the game sends numbers about each monster
 fight to usurper-reborn.net, including your class, level, stats and
 gear power, the monster's family and level, rounds, damage, potions,
 abilities and spells used, and XP and gold gained. No names, no chat,
 no save files. Uploads carry a random id that is not tied to your
 account. This is not anonymous: the id and your exact stats can link
 your fights. The answer covers every character on this copy of the
 game. You can change it at any time under Preferences. Rows already
 sent are kept for up to 30 days.
