Gold on hand and bank gold are separate balances. The Bank offers deposits, withdrawals and transfers alongside other banking activities.

## Spend deliberately

Keep enough on hand for healing, Mental treatment and travel needs. Compare the actual shop quote with your budget before confirming. The wiki's item value and sell value are built-in template fields, not universal transaction prices.

Death and Mental rescue can affect carried gold. A banking plan helps manage exposure, but the Bank has its own robbery and loan systems; a deposit is not a promise that every world event ignores it.

## Loans and transfers

Loans carry obligations and interest. Read the terms and balance messages rather than treating borrowed gold as a reward. Transfers affect another character and should be confirmed carefully.

Server state and treasury activity change over time. No live accounts or player wealth are exported into this wiki.
