Dungeon rooms can contain monsters, treasure, features and routes deeper. Progress is not only a damage check: supplies and Mental matter across the entire trip.

What you can meet in a room, from chests, traps and shrines to puzzles and strangers, is on [dungeon features and random events](/wiki/en/world/dungeon-features/). The rarest events have their own page, [rare encounters](/wiki/en/world/rare-encounters/).

## Entering and choosing a floor

The first time you enter, the game puts you on the floor equal to your level, or on the deepest floor open to you if that is lower. Later visits start on the floor you last stood on. On the floor overview, L changes the floor: type a floor number, or a step such as +1 or -1.

From floor 3 a regular encounter can hold an elite monster, and on floors 4 to 10 groups of up to three mix monster families. See [monsters](/wiki/en/monsters/#elites).

## Rest and exploration

New rooms add depth strain, while already explored rooms do not charge that room strain again. Completed monster fights add their own strain. Camp in a cleared room where the option is available. Camp and Safe Haven rest share a floor allowance. A room you cleared stays cleared after a disconnect, a reconnect or a save taken on the floor.

Broken prevents deeper travel. A portal is not a way around that condition. [Mental treatment](/wiki/en/characters/mental/) belongs in your expedition plan.

## Dungeon outposts

Some theme boundaries contain safe outposts with trading, healing or lore. Services vary by outpost, so do not assume that every stop can heal you or sells the same supplies.

These are dungeon destinations, distinct from the Settlement on Castle Grounds. Their lore can reveal deeper history; read it when you want that discovery.

## Boss readiness

Boss definitions use base values and phases. Your actual encounter can scale. Before committing, review equipment, consumables, resources and group condition. [Boss references](/wiki/en/monsters/bosses/) keep story and secret definitions behind a disclosure.
