## Main Street

The Inn, dungeon, exploration, Team Corner and Dark Alley form the central adventuring routes. Some menu actions appear only after progression.

## Merchant Row

Weapon, armor, magic and music shops serve different needs. Auctions are another market, not a guarantee that a particular built-in item is always for sale.

## Guild Row

The Healer treats injuries and conditions. The Temple handles gods and devotion. The Old Church offers confession. The Bank handles money, the Level Master advances and trains characters, and the Quest Hall offers work.

## Home and Hearth

Home, lodging and Love Street handle domestic and relationship activities. Home rest and sleep are useful Mental recovery sources.

## Castle Grounds

The Castle, challenges, Sanctum and Settlement cover political and later-world activities. Confirm consequences before challenging authority.

## Status and Notice Board

Status, progression, records, Fame and good deeds help you understand the character. News and World Events describe the changing town.

## Online district

Who's Online, chat, arena, world bosses and guilds expose shared-world features when online. For command behavior, read [online play](/wiki/en/online/).
