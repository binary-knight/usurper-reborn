Main Street shows a few places and a list of districts. Press a district's key to open it, then the place's key; R goes back. More places appear as you gain your first levels. The classic single-screen layout can be chosen in Settings (`~`).

## Main Street

D Dungeons, I Inn, E Explore, T Team Corner and A [Dark Alley](/wiki/en/world/dark-alley/).

## Merchant Row

Press M, then W Weapon Shop, A Armor Shop, M Magic Shop, U [Music Shop](/wiki/en/world/music-shop/) or J Auction House. See [shops](/wiki/en/items/shops/).

## Guild Row

Press G, then H [Healer](/wiki/en/world/healer/), T [Temple](/wiki/en/world/temple/), O [Old Church](/wiki/en/world/church/), B [Bank](/wiki/en/items/banking/), V Level Master or U Quest Hall.

## Home and Hearth

Press H, then H [Home](/wiki/en/world/home/), L Lodging or X [Love Street](/wiki/en/world/relationships/).

## Castle Grounds

Press C, then K [Castle](/wiki/en/world/castle/), C [Challenges on Anchor Road](/wiki/en/world/anchor-road/), S [Sanctum](/wiki/en/world/sanctum/) or E [Settlement](/wiki/en/world/settlement/), once it is built.

## Status and Notice Board

Press S for Status, Progress, Stats Record, Fame and Good Deeds. Press N for News and World Events.

## Online district

Online only: press O, then W Who's Online, C Chat, A [Arena](/wiki/en/online/arena/), B World Boss or G Guilds.

## Lodging

Lodging is the Dormitory. You can list and examine the other sleepers and sleep there. In single-player sleeping is free, restores you fully and starts a new day. Online a Dormitory bed costs {{balance:DormitorySleepCost}} gold; sleeping saves and logs you out, and leaves you open to attack while you sleep. Online you can also attack a sleeper for gold. The Inn also rents rooms online; see [the Inn](/wiki/en/world/rest/).

## Level Master

The Level Master raises your level with the experience you have earned. The master also teaches abilities and spells, shows your progression roadmap, trains proficiencies, sets your [specialization](/wiki/en/characters/specializations/) and lets you give experience to a spouse, teammate or companion. See [leveling](/wiki/en/characters/leveling/).

## Quest Hall

View, claim, turn in or abandon quests, with a daily claim limit, and read the bounty board. See [quests](/wiki/en/world/quests/).

## Team Corner

Create, join or leave a team, recruit NPCs for a daily wage, equip and resurrect members and view team rankings. The hire list shows the NPCs nearest your level first, hiring costs less below level 25, and the town keeps a pool of low-level NPCs to hire. Online adds team messages, player echoes, team wars and a headquarters. See [teams](/wiki/en/online/teams/).

## Auction House and News

Offline, the Auction House lets you list an item at your own price and buy other listings; a listing expires after 30 real days. Online, a listing runs 12, 24, 48 or 72 hours for a fee of 5, 4, 3 or 2 percent of the price plus the king's tax. The [shops guide](/wiki/en/items/shops/) has the details. News is the town's newsboard, newest first.

The Gym from older versions was removed and cannot be reached.
