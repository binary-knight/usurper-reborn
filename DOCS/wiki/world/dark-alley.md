The Dark Alley combines underground commerce, risky training, drugs, pickpocketing, gambling, the Pit, loans and Shadows faction services. Access and available services depend on your character and faction standing.

## Costs beyond gold

Drug highs can raise Mental temporarily but bring a crash, tolerance and addiction. Addiction lowers the ordinary Mental cap. [Rehab](/wiki/en/world/healer/) addresses that system; buying another high does not.

Theft, violence and other actions can affect alignment, law and your god's Favor. Umbrath rewards qualifying theft; Judicar treats it as a taboo.

## Loans and faction services

A loan is a debt, not free loot. Consider enforcement and repayment before accepting. Shadows recruitment, tribute, black-market access and informants use faction conditions; visiting the alley does not automatically unlock all of them.

## Read before confirming

A service can change your character or start a fight. Keep enough reserves for treatment and do not assume ordinary monster rewards or Mental rules apply to every exhibition.

This guide omits hidden easter eggs and exact hidden odds. Discover those only if you choose to pursue them in-game.
