The Settlement on Castle Grounds leads to the Outskirts, an NPC-built community beyond the city gates. It is not one of the safe outposts found inside the dungeon.

## Construction and contributions

NPC settlers pool resources and build structures. The location shows completed buildings, ongoing work and proposals. A contribution gives up gold to the settlement; check its current progress before spending.

Terran recognizes qualifying settlement donations as a deed. This uses your shared daily deed cap, not an unlimited Favor loop.

## Services depend on buildings

Completed structures can offer a tavern, shrine, workshop, watchtower, market and council services. Proposed buildings can add other activities such as a library or herbalist hut. A menu appearing in the source does not mean that every live settlement has already built it.

The library is one of the shared once-a-day learning recovery sources for Mental. Council rewards and gathering can interact with Terran's boon.

## Changing world state

The settlement develops with its population and resources. Check the live building list rather than expecting the wiki to prescribe a fixed construction order or treasury balance.
