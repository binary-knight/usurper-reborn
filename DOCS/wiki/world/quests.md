Quests have different origins: the Quest Hall, royal work, conversations and exploration can each lead to a goal. Check the active quest text rather than interpreting every rumor as an accepted task.

## Track the actual objective

A target, prerequisite and reward belong to the specific quest. Finishing a similar activity does not necessarily satisfy another quest. Return to the appropriate giver or use the quest menu when the game asks for it.

## Keep story discoveries in context

Some paths depend on choices and resolved encounters. This wiki does not put late outcomes or ending walkthroughs into search snippets. Secret boss definitions are available through an explicit disclosure in the [boss reference](/wiki/en/monsters/bosses/).

## Avoid guessed rewards

Item values and rarity do not identify a quest reward source. Use the displayed quest offer and actual completion message to confirm what your character earned.
