The Sanctum offers charitable and honor-oriented activities. It is separate from the Temple and the Castle's political menu.

## Charity

Alms, orphanage funding and hospice support are different actions. Read the donation and consequence messages before giving gold. A charitable contribution is not a shop purchase and should not be assumed to return equipment.

## Honor and recognition

The Hall of Heroes records recognition. The honor tournament is gated by renown; if it is absent or refused, check the current eligibility rather than trying a key copied from an older menu.

Prepare for tournament combat with the same care as other chained fights. Mental collapse can interrupt a chain before the next fight.

For character condition and recovery, see [Mental](/wiki/en/characters/mental/) and the [Healer](/wiki/en/world/healer/).
