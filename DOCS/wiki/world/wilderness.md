The wilderness offers regions, travel encounters and discoveries outside the dungeon. Region access and discoveries can depend on level and prior exploration.

## Prepare for a different trip

Use the current exploration menu to choose a direction and region. Events, travelers and discoveries are not guaranteed shopping lists. Take treatment and escape seriously even though wilderness fights do not charge dungeon depth strain.

Your first wilderness exploration of the day can restore Mental. Sylvana's boon improves wilderness rewards at its effective Favor strength.

## What the reference cannot promise

A built-in region definition does not establish that a named item always drops there. This wiki does not invent a drop source from flavor text or rarity. For fixed gear requirements, use the [equipment catalog](/wiki/en/items/).
