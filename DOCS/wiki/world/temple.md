The Temple is organized into rooms. Legacy shortcuts can point you to the new location for an action.

## Nave and Undercroft

The Nave holds the altars and worship, prayer, offerings and the Altars screen. The Undercroft serves the dark gods and holds desecration. Evil characters can enter the Temple, though priests can refuse inappropriate offerings to good gods. Choosing and switching gods is on [gods and faith](/wiki/en/gods/).

### How much Chivalry from a Temple sacrifice

A gold offering at an altar moves your alignment by the gold / 100, from 1 up to {{balance:MaxAlignmentGainPerTempleSacrifice}}. An offering to a god whose Darkness is above its Goodness (Umbrath, Mortis and Discordia) moves you toward Darkness and adds the same amount to your Shadows standing; any other god moves you toward Chivalry and adds it to your Faith standing. 3,000 gold to a good god gives 30 of each. Gold to your own god also buys [Favor](/wiki/en/gods/favor/#gold-offering-how-much-favor-for-gold) and, from 100 gold, a [sacrifice blessing](/wiki/en/gods/favor/#the-gold-sacrifice-blessing); gold to another god while you worship one records divine wrath.

### What does desecrating an altar do

Desecration in the Undercroft needs a dark deed left for the day, and you cannot desecrate your own god's altar or do it more than twice a day. It moves you 15 to 30 toward Darkness, gives level^1.5 x 20 experience and lowers that god's standing until the weekly reset. The first desecration of the day brings a curse 30 percent of the time, 10 to 29 + your level damage; the second always does, for three times the damage and the loss of 1 point of a random attribute among Strength, Dexterity, Constitution, Intelligence, Wisdom and Charisma. The dark gods count it as a deed; Terran counts it as a grave taboo.

## Faith and Old Stones

The Faith room holds the Faith faction's oath and cloister: you join the Faith there (see [factions](/wiki/en/world/castle/#factions-the-crown-the-shadows-and-the-faith)).

### What does the Inner Sanctum give

The Inner Sanctum is for members of the Faith: once a day, for {{balance:InnerSanctumCost}} gold, it raises one of nine attributes by 1 for good, chosen at random among Strength, Defense, Stamina, Agility, Charisma, Dexterity, Wisdom, Intelligence and Constitution.

### Settling a betrayal

While a betrayal can be settled, the Faith room also lists B: forgive a betrayer, take revenge on the Stranger once if she escaped her fight, or, for a companion who left you, make one act of atonement a day for gold. An act of atonement costs 100 gold per character level, at least 100, and counts as a kindness toward that companion's forgiveness. A forgiven companion waits at the Inn with loyalty 50. How resentment builds is on [companions](/wiki/en/combat/companions/#resentment-and-leaving).

Old Stones holds prophecies and visions. These are story-facing spaces, not a second Favor currency.

## Memory and deeper rooms

Halls of Memory and other rooms appear when their prerequisites apply. Absence from the current menu can be intentional progression, not a missing installation file.

Confession belongs at the [Church](/wiki/en/world/church/#how-much-does-confession-cost), not the Temple. Read [Favor](/wiki/en/gods/favor/) before worshipping, switching or giving up equipment.

:::spoiler Late Temple boss access
The Deep Temple opens for the eligible late story fight. Follow the game's quest and readiness messages; a dungeon pointer is not the fight itself. Resolved story encounters are remembered rather than being offered as ordinary repeatable altar actions.
:::
