The Castle is a political and legal location, not just a shop. Rulership, prison orders and crime can affect other characters and your god's Favor.

## Crime has consequences

Theft and murder can be taboos. Self-defense against a street attacker is distinguished from murder. Starting a fight or surrendering to guards can move you into arrest and prison rules.

Read confirmation prompts. Arrest combat and surrender have consequences beyond the ordinary monster-fight rewards; exact hidden execution odds are not exposed in this guide.

## Prison

Prison has its own activities and escape or walk rules. Jailed characters do not follow the normal Mental-collapse path. A character sent to prison should use that location's current menu rather than assume normal town travel is still available.

## Rulership

Judicar can reward appropriate prison orders, but political power does not remove the need to consider consequences. The [god pages](/wiki/en/gods/) list deeds and taboos, not a moral guarantee that an action is safe.
