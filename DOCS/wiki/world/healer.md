The Healer treats more than missing HP. Decide whether you need wound treatment, supplies, addiction rehab or Mental therapy.

## Talk Therapy

Therapy restores Mental and clears Broken. The bill depends on missing Mental, your level and the Healer's tax. A Broken character has a minimum bill even if ordinary recovery has already raised Mental.

Check the quote and carried gold before confirming. Therapy can lift Mental above the addiction cap, but that surplus does not permanently remove the cap.

## Addiction Rehab

Rehab is offered to addicted characters. It clears addiction, cancels a pending drug crash, resets tolerance and also clears Broken. If you are not addicted, Talk Therapy is the usual Broken cure.

## Willow Draught

Willow Draught is a separate supply for the dungeon. It restores {{balance:MentalWillowDraughtGain}} Mental up to your cap, not HP. The purchase price is based on healing potion cost and tax. Read the capacity message when stocking up.

For the full recovery rules, see [Mental](/wiki/en/characters/mental/).
