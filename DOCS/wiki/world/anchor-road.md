Anchor Road is the town's challenge ground. From Main Street open the Castle Grounds district with C, then press C for Challenges. It appears once the full town menu unlocks.

## Challenges

- **B Bounty hunting:** fight a townsperson with a dark reputation, or one near your level. It uses your daily player fights.
- **G Gang war:** your team fights another team. You need a team and team fights left for the day.
- **T The Gauntlet:** a run of monster waves, once a day, for an entry fee that grows with your level. Every wave pays gold and experience, the last wave pays a bonus, and you recover some HP and mana between waves. Finishing the Gauntlet at a high level grants a small lasting damage and defence bonus. The Gauntlet has its own achievements.
- **M Sellsword Hall:** take freelance contracts, a few a day. Completed contracts raise your sellsword rank, which raises your pay. The count of completed contracts carries over into [New Game Plus](/wiki/en/characters/new-game-plus/).

## Town control

With a team you can press C to claim the town for your team, or F to give up town control.

## Prison grounds

P leads to the prison grounds outside the cells. You can view the prisoners, or try a jailbreak. A successful jailbreak is a daring good deed that raises Chivalry and makes the news. A failed one costs a share of your HP and adds Darkness.

## Status and return

S shows your challenge status, and R returns to Main Street.

The Gauntlet is not the [Arena](/wiki/en/online/arena/), which is online player combat.
