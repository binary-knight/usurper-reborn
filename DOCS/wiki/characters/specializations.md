Specialization becomes available at the Level Master from level {{balance:SpecializationUnlockLevel}}, for classes that have defined options. The first choice is free; changing it later costs {{balance:SpecializationRespecCost}} gold.

## What changes

A specialization adds extra stat growth on every future level-up and a few combat bonuses. It is not a retroactive rebuild of the character's earlier levels, and it does not teach any new ability or spell. Choose around your intended role, such as damage, healing, support or tanking.

Each [class page](/wiki/en/characters/classes/) lists its specialization descriptions, role and per-level bonuses. An empty list means the exporter found no specialization defined for that class; it does not imply a missing translation.

## Healer specializations

Holy (Paladin), Restoration (Cleric), Mystic (Sage), Minstrel (Bard), Apothecary (Alchemist) and Spiritwalker (Mystic Shaman) are healer specializations. They add 20 percent to the healing of your abilities and spells, though not to the Mystic Shaman's Healing Totem, Spirit Link Totem or Ancestral Guidance.

## Tank specializations

Each tank specialization strengthens one level 40 tank ability of its class:

- Protection (Warrior): Shield Wall Formation reduces incoming damage by 40 percent instead of 30.
- Guardian (Paladin): Divine Mandate reflects 20 percent of damage instead of 15.
- Juggernaut (Barbarian): Rage Challenge restores 8 percent of maximum HP per round instead of 5.

With a shield equipped, Protection adds 20 percent and Guardian 15 percent to the damage of every class ability. Guardian with a shield also heals 15 percent more with abilities. Juggernaut gets no shield bonus.

## Teammates

An NPC teammate's specialization also steers its combat choices: some specializations stop it using certain kinds of abilities, set how hurt an ally must be before it heals, and decide whether it acts as the party's healer or tank.

## Before switching

Check the displayed price and confirm that the new role fits the weapons, armor and actions you use. A role name describes an intended style, not a guarantee that every fight will suit it.
