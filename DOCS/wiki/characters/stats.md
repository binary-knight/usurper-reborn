## Offensive and defensive attributes

- Strength contributes to physical damage.
- Dexterity contributes to accuracy, ranged damage and critical hits.
- Agility contributes to dodging and combat speed.
- Defence and armor reduce incoming damage through the combat system; neither makes you immune.

## Resources and casting

- HP is your current health. Maximum HP is your capacity, not your current health.
- Constitution contributes to HP, poison and disease resistance, and stamina recovery.
- Intelligence supports spell power and mana.
- Wisdom supports magic-related effects and resistance.
- Stamina pays for many martial actions; mana pays for spells and some abilities.

Charisma supports social and economic interactions. The meaning of a bonus depends on the action that reads it; do not assume every attribute is used in every menu.

## Permanent versus temporary

Base stats, equipped stats and current resources are different. A permanent reward belongs to your base character; a gear bonus disappears when you remove that gear. Boons, drugs, status effects and combat buffs can change the current outcome.

See [leveling](/wiki/en/characters/leveling/) for growth and [Mental](/wiki/en/characters/mental/) for a separate condition that changes combat performance.
