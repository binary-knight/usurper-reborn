Use the Notice Board for news and World Events, and the status-oriented menus for records and Fame. These screens describe a live world rather than fixed release data.

## Events can affect play

Disasters and other events can alter town activity. Reading news during an active disaster can cause the daily witnessing Mental loss. Repeatedly reading is not a way to multiply that daily event.

## Rankings

A ranking reflects its own measure. Temple standing uses devotion and townsfolk faith; Fame and other records use different systems. Do not read one board as proof of being strongest in every fight.

Weekly divine standing chooses the god of the week. Check the Temple for the current winner rather than relying on a guide's example.

## Use current information

The [website](/) exposes public server information. The wiki deliberately does not export leaderboards, private accounts or transient event state.
