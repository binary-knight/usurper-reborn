Use the Notice Board for news and World Events, and the status-oriented menus for records and Fame. These screens describe a live world rather than fixed release data.

## How often do world events happen

In single-player each new game day has a 30 percent chance to start a world event, drawn from the events the season and the town's state allow. Every 7th day also forces a Tournament Day, a Merchant Caravan or a Harvest Festival, and every 30th day a Royal Festival, a Royal Bounty, a Gold Rush or an Ancient Relic. Each event lasts its number of days, counted down at each new day.

Online the server's daily reset does not start or count down world events. The one event online play adds is The Realm Celebrates, after a world boss falls, and it does not count down either.

## What does each world event do

An event can change shop prices, the experience and the gold of a monster fight you win. Several events at once multiply, and the totals are held to x0.25 to x4.0 for prices, x0.5 to x3.0 for experience and x0.25 to x3.0 for gold. The prices are those of the Weapon, Armor and Magic shops and the Dark Alley; the Healer shows the change but does not charge it. The last column shows when the daily roll can draw the event. Of the events marked No, only the Gold Rush and The Realm Celebrates come another way (above); the others never start.

A plague has effects of its own: while it is active a healthy character in a fight takes 1 percent of maximum HP 10 percent of the rounds, with a small chance to catch the plague, and a sick one takes 25 percent more disease damage. The stat changes some events announce are not applied to anyone.

## Events can affect your Mental

Disasters (a plague, a cursed land, a bandit raid, a monster invasion, a demon portal or a dragon sighting) can cause the daily witnessing Mental loss when you read the news. Repeatedly reading is not a way to multiply that daily event.

## Rankings

A ranking reflects its own measure. Temple standing uses devotion and townsfolk faith; Fame and other records use different systems. Do not read one board as proof of being strongest in every fight.

Weekly divine standing chooses the god of the week, whose followers gain more combat experience until the next week (see [player-gods](/wiki/en/gods/player-gods/#standing-and-rankings)). Check the Temple for the current winner rather than relying on a guide's example.
