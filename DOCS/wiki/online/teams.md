Teams support adventuring relationships and group participation. Guilds are a separate online social system. Joining one does not imply that every member is automatically following you in combat.

A new team or guild name cannot contain < > & or a double quote. The game says so, creates nothing and takes no fee. Existing team and guild names are kept.

## Before grouping

Recruit or invite through the available menu, and check who is actually grouped. Different teammate policies apply to players, NPCs and other followers.

Bring complementary actions rather than only damage. Healing, wards and tanking matter when a party is taking different kinds of pressure.

## Hiring NPCs

At Team Corner you hire NPCs for a price and a daily wage.

- The per-level part of the price is the lower of 2,000 gold per level and 80 gold times the level squared. It is lower below level 25 and the same from level 25 up. The NPC's Strength, Defence and Agility add to the price, and your relationship with the NPC changes it further.
- An NPC above your level costs 5 percent more for each level above you, up to 50 percent more at 10 levels above.
- The daily wage is the lower of 100 gold per level and 4 gold times the level squared. The same wage is charged for NPCs already on your team.
- The hire list shows the NPCs nearest your level first, within each relationship group.
- The town keeps a pool of up to 18 NPCs without a team to hire: six each at levels 1 to 5, 6 to 10 and 11 to 15.
- An NPC on your team does not age while it serves you. An NPC past its lifespan dies seven days after leaving your team.
- A hired NPC carries the oath-mark of your banner: see [groups](/wiki/en/combat/groups/#the-oath-mark).

## Conditions remain personal

Grouped players keep their own Mental, Favor, resources and daily limits. A leader's Miracle use does not consume every player's daily Miracle, and a healthy leader does not protect a Broken follower from collapse.

See [groups and support](/wiki/en/combat/groups/) for combat behavior.
