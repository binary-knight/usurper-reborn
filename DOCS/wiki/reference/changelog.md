Version **1.2.6** added detailed combat logging for the coming difficulty pass, made logins stop showing whether an account exists, refused < > & and the double quote in new names, made the website escape all player and sponsor text, and gave the balance dashboard Difficulty and Onboarding tabs. Version **1.2.5** translated the rest of the game, including the town, the castle, the online server, NPC speech, items and monsters, made every message, shop and status row fit 79 columns, stopped the nightly world boss mail, brought the Mysterious Stranger into play, and fixed PvP salvage, moral paradox saves and Magic Shop enchant removal. Version **1.2.4** made reconnecting keep you online, added /history for chat, made homes private, showed group combat in each player's language, added Magic Shop haggling over rings and necklaces and a letter from a neglected spouse, and fixed mail and immortal ascension. Version **1.2.3** translated the dungeon, its settlements, wilderness and rare encounters, made screens wrap at 79 columns, and gave Magic Shop basic rings and necklaces a small stat. Version **1.2.2** translated combat, the item comparison screens and status effects, and changed Temple offerings so they need no good deed and give none back. Version **1.2.1** opened this wiki and the Discord Ask and Suggest commands, and made NPC stat gains and the Iron Rations max HP bonus last. Version **1.2.0** brought Mental health, the rebuilt Temple, Favor, domain boons and permanent stat rewards.
