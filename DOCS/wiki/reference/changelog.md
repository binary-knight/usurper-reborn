This page lists each release as a short summary, newest first, with a link to its release notes.

Version **1.3.7** brought 18 changes: Power Hat, the Alchemist's late bombs and Corrosive Cloud, the Voidreaver's Constitution and Corruption Harvest tuned from the measurements of every class, monsters on floors 76 to 100 hitting harder, and Manwe's Creator's Ward stopping part of what gear enchantments add, Godbane held or not. Version **1.3.6** brought 79 changes: five enchant rules, among them a real magic resistance and poison over several rounds, an audit of every ability and spell of all seventeen classes so each does what its text says, and Last Stand and Death's Door that keep you fighting at 1 HP. Version **1.3.5** brought 46 changes: three counter enchants at the Magic Shop, Sunbane, Stonebane and Godbane, break the divine armor of the last three Old Gods and hold back two of each god's abilities, Maelketh's health scales with party size and Manwe's HP came down so a full party holding Godbane can win, many enchant, loot effect and combat faults were fixed, and crit damage, regeneration and armor piercing on gear were reworked. Version **1.3.4** brought 27 changes: a forgotten password can be reset by email from the game or the website, with an optional email address, never sold or shared, asked at registration or once at the next login, new passwords need at least 8 characters, and spells and Old God abilities that did not do what their text said were fixed, among them Probability Shift, Echo of Tomorrow, Doom in the early Old God fights and Manwe's named abilities. Version **1.3.3** brought 15 changes: dungeon floors 25 to 100 grow harder with depth and are built for a full team of five, and allies at 0 HP are Downed, can be rescued with a healing potion and are lost only when a monster finishes them. Version **1.3.2** brought 72 changes: class combat bonuses that apply in fights, god, Blood Price and divine wrath effects on basic attacks, monster ability fixes, online world events, NPC party gear that stays put, and a wiki rewritten with exact numbers. Version **1.3.1** fixed a load that could treat a current character as a save from before base stats existed, which replaced its base stats with its saved totals and equipped an item. Version **1.3.0** took the first step of a longer rework of dungeon difficulty: monsters choose their abilities with more sense, dungeon floors 4 to 10 mix monster families in groups of up to three, elite monsters with named traits appear from floor 3, physical attacks on monsters roll to hit and healing potions heal at least 20 percent of maximum HP. It also brought betrayal into play, made each dual-wield swing use the weapon in its own hand, lowered NPC hire prices below level 25, gave hired NPCs the oath-mark, and replaced the saved Online Play password with a login token. Version **1.2.7** added opt-in combat data sharing, asked once and off by default on a BBS or server, gave the balance dashboard a Source choice for the rows it receives, and fixed the gap in a monster's critical hit line. Version **1.2.6** added detailed combat logging for the coming difficulty pass, made logins stop showing whether an account exists, refused < > & and the double quote in new names, made the website escape all player and sponsor text, and gave the balance dashboard Difficulty and Onboarding tabs. Version **1.2.5** translated the rest of the game, including the town, the castle, the online server, NPC speech, items and monsters, made every message, shop and status row fit 79 columns, stopped the nightly world boss mail, brought the Mysterious Stranger into play, and fixed PvP salvage, moral paradox saves and Magic Shop enchant removal. Version **1.2.4** made reconnecting keep you online, added /history for chat, made homes private, showed group combat in each player's language, added Magic Shop haggling over rings and necklaces and a letter from a neglected spouse, and fixed mail and immortal ascension. Version **1.2.3** translated the dungeon, its settlements, wilderness and rare encounters, made screens wrap at 79 columns, and gave Magic Shop basic rings and necklaces a small stat. Version **1.2.2** translated combat, the item comparison screens and status effects, and changed Temple offerings so they need no good deed and give none back. Version **1.2.1** opened this wiki and the Discord Ask and Suggest commands, and made NPC stat gains and the Iron Rations max HP bonus last. Version **1.2.0** brought Mental health, the rebuilt Temple, Favor, domain boons and permanent stat rewards.
