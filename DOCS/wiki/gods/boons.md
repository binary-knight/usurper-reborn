Boons are domain effects, not another class. Favor scales their strength, and prayer can temporarily strengthen them. An effect only helps in the context that actually uses it.

## Fighting and healing

- [Solarius](/wiki/en/gods/solarius/) improves damage against undead and demons, and healing while fighting them. This includes the applicable spells, abilities, potions and herbs.
- [Valorian](/wiki/en/gods/valorian/) improves damage while you are below the HP threshold. Full health does not activate that low-health benefit.
- [Amara](/wiki/en/gods/amara/) strengthens your heals on yourself and allies, including bard songs, and the party wards you raise.
- [Judicar](/wiki/en/gods/judicar/) reduces damage from monsters and improves bounty rewards. The protection is not a promise that every attack can be blocked.
- [Umbrath](/wiki/en/gods/umbrath/) improves critical chance and Dark Alley pickpocketing. Critical rules and caps still belong to the combat system.
- [Arcanus](/wiki/en/gods/arcanus/) improves spell damage and mana recovered each round. A bigger mana pool and mana recovery are different effects.
- [Discordia](/wiki/en/gods/discordia/) improves damage against players and can make a monster fail its first action. The first-action effect is not an unlimited hold on that monster.

## Survival and rewards

- [Terran](/wiki/en/gods/terran/) increases maximum HP and improves garden herbs and settlement council share. A larger maximum is not the same as a free full heal.
- [Mortis](/wiki/en/gods/mortis/) reduces gold lost when you die. Its daily death-saving Miracle is a separate Chosen benefit, not something every Follower gets.
- [Sylvana](/wiki/en/gods/sylvana/) improves gold and experience from wilderness activity. It does not double all rewards from every location.

## Read the actual strength

Individual god pages show the exported base constants. The [Favor table](/wiki/en/gods/favor/) shows tier strength. Player-gods use their chosen domain with an additional standing and inactivity scale, so their current effective strength can differ from a canonical god.

God wards protect Mental and are separate from these boons. A domain's damage or reward benefit does not automatically erase grief, strain or withdrawal.
