An immortal player-god chooses a domain. Followers receive its domain boon, scaled by the god's standing against the strongest Temple god, alongside configured immortal boons. Activity matters: a player-god's boon can fade when the immortal stops playing.

## Standing

Standing combines follower Favor and townsfolk faith. Temple gods and player-gods share the rankings. The god of the week grants followers an experience benefit until the next weekly reset.

These values change with the live world. The wiki does not export player names, follower lists or standings.

## Divine deeds

An immortal can bless followers, chastise them and recruit townsfolk. Blessing grants Favor and a tier-sensitive combat blessing within its limits; chastising removes Favor. These actions do not always succeed on an unreachable offline follower.

Your own [Favor](/wiki/en/gods/favor/) still determines tiers and daily Miracle access. A player's godhood is not a second simultaneous worship slot.
