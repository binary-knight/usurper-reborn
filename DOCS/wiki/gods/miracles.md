At Chosen, your god offers a daily Miracle. The combat menu shows it when it would help. Each grouped player has their own use. Saving and reloading does not restore a spent Miracle.

## Healing and resources

- Amara heals you and living allies to full.
- Terran restores your HP to full.
- Arcanus refills your mana.

## Offense, control and escape

- Solarius banishes an eligible undead or demon. Bosses are not banished; the Miracle deals holy damage instead.
- Valorian makes your next hit a certain critical.
- Judicar binds a foe; bosses use their control resistance rules.
- Umbrath removes you from the fight without the ordinary flee penalties, including the Mental and Favor losses.
- Sylvana calls a spirit wolf for that fight.
- Discordia confuses foes; boss resistance still applies.

## Mortis and death protection

Mortis acts automatically when an eligible monster-fight death would occur and the daily Miracle is available. You survive at minimal HP instead. This protection does not apply to arrest or exhibition fights.

## Where Miracles do not work

Miracles are not available in player fights or world boss fights. Do not plan those encounters around a Miracle key that belongs to another combat context.

A Miracle does not replace supplies or [Mental treatment](/wiki/en/characters/mental/). Its daily use must be available, your Favor must meet the tier, and its effect must be useful in the current fight.
