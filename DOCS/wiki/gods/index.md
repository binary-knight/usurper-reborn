You worship one god at a time. The Temple's canonical gods and player-gods compete for followers. Your personal Favor controls how much of your god's boon you receive.

## Build a relationship

Use [Favor, prayer and sacrifice](/wiki/en/gods/favor/) for tiers, devotion, wards and Miracles. A god's individual page lists its generated boon constants, deeds and taboos.
Read [what each boon does](/wiki/en/gods/boons/) and [daily Miracles](/wiki/en/gods/miracles/) for their actual combat and reward contexts.

The Temple's rankings describe a changing online world, not a fixed table in this wiki. Player-god domains use the same canonical domain mechanics but have additional strength and activity rules.
