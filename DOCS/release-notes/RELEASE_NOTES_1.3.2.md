# Usurper Reborn v1.3.2

## Changelist

### Classes

• Adjusted the Warrior, Assassin, Barbarian, Mystic Shaman, Paladin and Ranger class combat bonuses, which now apply in fights
• Adjusted Backstab [K] to the Assassin only, now shown in the combat menu
• Improved Smite [Z] for the Paladin, now in the combat menu and spending a daily charge
• Fixed the Mystic Shaman regaining no mana each round and never auto-drinking mana potions
• Adjusted the Mystic Shaman to follow its own weapon and armor rules
• Fixed prestige class spells starting below Average
• Fixed some class passives missing from the Status screen

### Combat

• Adjusted critical hits so every crit bonus stops at the same ceiling
• Adjusted world boss attacks to use the same critical hit chance as other fights
• Fixed temple god damage boons, prayer and sacrifice blessing damage and the Old God echo not applying to basic attacks
• Fixed Umbrath's critical hit boon not applying to basic attacks
• Fixed the Blood Price damage penalty not applying in fights
• Fixed boss slayer gear not adding damage against bosses outside world boss fights
• Fixed divine wrath's combat penalty having no effect
• Fixed party members not regaining stamina and mana during combat
• Fixed the difficulty healing bonus applying only to auto-combat potions
• Fixed basic attack proficiency not improving through combat use
• Fixed Dispel Evil not removing an enraged boss's extra Strength, Defence and attacks
• Fixed the Outskirts Workshop and Arena damage buffs having no effect in fights

### Monsters and difficulty

• Adjusted difficulty so Easy, Hard and Nightmare change monster XP and gold once instead of twice
• Fixed summon, flee and mana drain monster abilities also dealing a full hit
• Fixed the Thorns monster ability doing nothing

### Dungeon and Outskirts

• Fixed dungeon outposts, secret bosses, lore libraries, memory rooms and meditation chambers being replaced by random room events
• Fixed a failed dungeon riddle charging its damage twice
• Fixed the Outskirts Prison buff not reducing room and chest trap damage
• Fixed the Herbalist Hut allowing retries and the Mystic Circle using its day for nothing
• Fixed the Sellsword contract Bandits on the Trade Road being impossible to finish
• Fixed the dungeon entry fee discount and surcharge going to the wrong allies
• Fixed free allies staying behind when your gold could not cover every entry fee
• Improved dungeon entry to name a saved party member who is dead

### Gods and story

• Fixed an alliance with Noctura not handing over the Shadow Crown
• Fixed saving Manwe offering a relic you already hold
• Fixed several ending conditions that did not count a spared or defeated god correctly
• Fixed the ending screen counting different gods than the ending itself
• Improved grief so each finished grief for a story companion grants Wisdom
• Fixed Evil characters being able to enter the Sanctum

### Town, shops and items

• Fixed the Status screen listing Blood Price shop and healer markups that do not exist
• Fixed several enchant, pet and passive descriptions that did not match the game

### Party and NPCs

• Fixed party Clerics wasting turns casting Purify as a heal
