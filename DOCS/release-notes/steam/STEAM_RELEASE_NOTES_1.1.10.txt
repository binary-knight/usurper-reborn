[h1]Usurper Reborn 1.1.10[/h1]

A player met Mael'Keth after floors that went down in one or two turns and lost three companions in one fight. This release is about that fight, the companions in it, and several smaller reports from the same conversation.

[list]
[*]Mael'Keth is tuned so a level-30s party can win: 22,000 HP and 265 Strength. Our fight log showed no party below level 48 had ever beaten him. A level-32 party now wins a little under half its fights, and he still kills companions.
[*]The five floors before every Old God grow tougher, up to +25% HP and damage on the floor before the god, so the god is not the first real fight in five floors.
[*]A god's War Cry and similar buffs last three rounds instead of the whole fight, and the intro screen shows the HP the god really has.
[*]Dialogue answers before a god fight now do what they promise.
[*]Monster special attacks on companions are softened like normal hits, a god's ability aimed at a companion is its normal attack, and tanks protect themselves before they taunt.
[*]Items waiting for room in your pack reach alt characters, can no longer be lost, arrive when you open /boss, and team bequests reach the team's leader.
[*]The Inn lists every patron, not just the first eight, and you can find someone by name.
[*]In PvP, frost slows instead of freezing, and stuns, sleeps and freezes can no longer be chained.
[*]Team HQ upgrades cost what the menu shows.
[/list]
