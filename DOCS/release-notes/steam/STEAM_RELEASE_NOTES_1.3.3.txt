[h2]Changelist[/h2]
[h3]Monsters and difficulty[/h3]
[list]
[*]Adjusted dungeon monster groups from level 25 to grow a little each level instead of jumping at levels 31 and 61
[*]Adjusted dungeon monsters past level 50 to keep growing with every level
[*]Adjusted dungeon monsters from level 25 to gain HP and damage with each level, built for a full team of five
[/list]
[h3]Party and NPCs[/h3]
[list]
[*]Adjusted companions, NPC allies, spouses, lovers and fighting beasts at 0 HP in a fight to be Downed instead of killed
[*]Improved combat with Rescue (U), which lifts a Downed ally with one healing potion
[*]Adjusted Rescue in an Old God fight to follow the potion cooldown
[*]Adjusted predator monsters and bosses to announce a finish on a Downed ally and land it on a later turn
[*]Adjusted an announced finish to stop on a rescue, a taunt, a hold or the monster's death
[*]Adjusted allies still Downed at the end of a fight to get back up with half their HP
[*]Adjusted permadeath, grief, loyalty loss and the left to die penalty to apply only when a monster finishes an ally
[*]Adjusted the left to die penalty to count only a Downed ally you held a potion for and did not rescue
[*]Adjusted Downed companions so they can no longer sacrifice themselves to save you
[*]Improved the allies box and the after-fight party summary to show Downed allies, downs and rescues
[/list]
