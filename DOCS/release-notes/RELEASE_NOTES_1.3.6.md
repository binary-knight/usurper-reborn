# Usurper Reborn v1.3.6

## Changelist

### Enchants and gear

• Improved magic resistance so it reduces damage from magical monster attacks and from spells in PvP, by up to half, except Old God attacks
• Improved weapon poison so a proc poisons the monster for 4 rounds and a new proc refreshes it, in place of a single hit
• Fixed the poison proc message naming Venomstrike for every poison weapon
• Adjusted Titan's Resolve to give 5 percent more max HP in place of 5 percent more defense
• Improved companions' thorns gear so it hurts monsters that strike them
• Improved Boss Slayer so it also adds its damage to Power Attack and class abilities
• Improved weapon enchants and lifesteal so they work in PvP and against the world boss
• Improved Phoenix Fire to add 30 weapon power to newly enchanted weapons and a 30 percent chance of fire damage worth a quarter of the hit
• Improved Frostbite to add 30 weapon power to newly enchanted weapons and a 25 percent chance to slow
• Adjusted Phoenix Fire and Frostbite to go on weapons only, one of each element per weapon
• Fixed the Phoenix Fire description leaving out its chance and the Hungarian Phoenix Fire and Frostbite texts naming Strength
• Fixed the fire and frost proc messages naming Phoenix Fire and Frostbite for every weapon

### Combat rules

• Fixed area abilities asking for a target they do not use, and Seal Fracture hitting a random monster
• Adjusted area abilities so a dual wielder gets no off-hand swing after them
• Fixed attack and defense bonuses from different sources wiping each other, so the highest one holds and the next returns when it ends
• Adjusted attack and defense bonuses from different sources to no longer add together, so the highest one applies as the guide says
• Fixed recasting a bonus ability or spell, such as Flametongue or Haste, stacking its bonus again
• Fixed Fear, Soul Rend, Intimidate and Dissonant Whispers holding a boss or Old God round after round, so a boss now resists half the time, is held at most one round and resists more on reuse
• Fixed Primal Scream, Carnival of Chaos, Causality Loop, Mind Spike, Psychic Scream, Deadly Joke and Last Laugh confusing bosses with no resist, so a boss now shrugs it off a quarter of the time and stays confused at most two rounds
• Fixed Frost Touch and Ice Storm slowing bosses for the full length
• Fixed marks wearing off once per action in place of once per round
• Adjusted Disappearing Act and Escape to follow the flee rules, with no escape where fleeing is off and a lower chance against bosses and Old Gods
• Fixed poison from Poison Touch, Poison Blade, Biaxin and Abyssal Eruption ignoring a boss's phase immunity and divine armor
• Fixed Searing Totem, Shadow Step, Void Bolt, Paradox Collapse, Pillar of Fire and Corrupting Touch extra damage ignoring a boss's phase immunity and divine armor
• Fixed Weaken's defense cut doing nothing, so a weakened monster now takes more damage from every hit
• Fixed monsters hitting harder after they are poisoned
• Fixed Grand Finale and ten other area abilities' damage missing from the fight's damage totals and from Paradox Collapse's bonus
