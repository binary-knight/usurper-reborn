# Usurper Reborn v1.3.7

## Changelist

### Class tuning

• Adjusted Power Hat to heal at least 20 percent of max HP, rising to 50 percent at level 100
• Adjusted the Philosopher's Bomb, Cataclysm Bomb and Corrosive Cloud damage
• Adjusted the Voidreaver to gain 1.5 Constitution a level, with existing Voidreavers given what their levels would have gained, and Unmaking to cost 10 percent of current HP instead of 25
• Adjusted Corruption Harvest to heal at each poisoned kill and the Abysswarden level loss and NPC tables to 3 Constitution a level
• Fixed Void Hunger and Soul Eater so they act at each kill during the fight

### The deep dungeon and Manwe

• Adjusted deep monster damage to rise after level 75 to 10 percent higher by level 80
• Adjusted Manwe so the Creator's Ward still stops 55 percent of what gear enchantments add after a hit, his counter held or not
• Fixed the Warrior and Barbarian room feature attack bonus so it lasts into the next fight

### Gear and items

• Fixed Titan's Resolve max HP not showing after equip or unequip
• Fixed the Magic Shop silently leaving an enchant tag off a long item name
• Fixed a loot item losing its effect line after being worn

### Screens and texts

• Fixed combat quickbar rows that ran past 79 columns or out of their box in screen reader and boxed modes
• Fixed long combat menu rows running past the box in Spanish, Hungarian and Italian
• Fixed the Wisdom description on character creation to match what Wisdom does
• Fixed spell descriptions on the spell learning screens running past 79 columns
• Fixed the wiki guides, tables and labels to spell defense as the game text does

### Admin and tools

• Fixed the admin level edit to give the same gains as a level up for every class
• Improved the save editor with a toggle for the Voidreaver Constitution marker
